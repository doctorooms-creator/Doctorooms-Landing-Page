    53→### Technical & Performance Risks
    54→- GSAP ScrollTrigger pinning on mobile is risky → mobile uses vertical sequences, no pinning.
    55→- Heavy simultaneous layers → code-split, transform/opacity only, lazy-init GSAP client-side.
    56→- `prefers-reduced-motion` must fall back to CSS-revealed content (no info locked behind JS animation).
    57→- SSR: all animated sections are `'use client'`; GSAP registered inside `useIsomorphicLayoutEffect` within `useGSAP`/`gsap.context`.
