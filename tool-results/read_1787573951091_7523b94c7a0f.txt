   395→   395→   * inline script in <head>) so the SSR markup cannot flash. If JS is
   396→   396→   * disabled or fails to load, html.js is never added and the content
   397→   397→   * stays visible (good for SEO / no-JS accessibility).
   398→   398→   *
   399→   399→   * GSAP overrides these inline as soon as its `from()` tween runs.
